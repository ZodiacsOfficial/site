May 10 comes with a wrinkle the neighboring dates don't have: it
sits almost exactly on the line between the second and third decans
of [Taurus](/taurus/). Depending on your birth year — and sometimes
your birth hour — the Sun was either finishing the Mercury-ruled
middle of the sign or newly arrived in the Saturn-ruled last third.
Same steady Bull either way; the seasoning differs.

## Two flavors of one steadiness

On the Mercury side, the steadiness is articulate: list-making,
observant, quick to explain the method. On the Saturn side it turns
structural: quieter, more ambitious, oriented to duty and the long
build. Most May 10 people recognize both descriptions and live
somewhere between — a practical communicator with an unusually
grown-up spine. The [Taurus Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-taurus/)
supplies the constant: slow, loyal, sensory, unrushable.

## Loyal either way it leans

In love, both tones point at commitment by different roads: the
Mercury lean courts through conversation and thoughtful logistics,
the Saturn lean through responsibility and the quiet moving of the
relationship onto solid ground. Work rewards the blend — project
management, skilled trades with paperwork, planning roles with a
human face. Whatever the field, May 10 ends up trusted with the part
that must not fail.

## The weight both tones carry

The shared friction is over-weighting. The Mercury lean overthinks
the details; the Saturn lean over-carries the load; either way,
lightness gets rationed like a scarce material. The growth edge is
un-serious time on purpose — play that produces nothing, rest that
isn't earned first. The work will keep. That was the point of
building it so well.

Which decan is actually yours is arithmetic, not folklore: the

degree for your birth date, time, and place, and settles it in one
pass — along with the Moon, rising, and houses the date can't
supply.
