Some people treat time as an enemy; May 13 treats it as a coworker.

## Outlasting as a strategy

Talent gets the headlines; duration wins the categories that matter,
and May 13 knows it in the bone. This is the classmate still
practicing in year six, the founder still solvent in year ten.
Saturn's decan strips Taurus persistence of its comfort-seeking
excuse and points it at goals. The [Taurus
Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-taurus/) underneath keeps the process
humane — meals still get cooked, sleep still gets protected.
Endurance, here, runs on sustainable fuel.

## Devotion on a long lease

Partners of a May 13 report the same discovery: the early reserve
was load testing. Affection deepens annually, quietly compounding

only extends the timeline. At work, hand this date the long project
everyone else fears: turnarounds, restorations, research with
distant payoffs. Check back in two years; it will still be moving.

## Mistaking endurance for aliveness

The friction: the capacity to endure anything includes things that
shouldn't be endured. May 13 can outlast a bad job, a cold marriage,
a city it hates, filing the misery under weather. The growth edge is
auditing what the persistence is for — twice a year, asking not "can
I keep going?" (always yes) but "toward what?". Quitting the wrong
marathon is also a finish line.
