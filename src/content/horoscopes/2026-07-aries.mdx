July runs on your ruler. Mars spends the month in Gemini — your solar third
house of errands, messages, and neighborhood logistics, reading your Sun
sign as the first house the way monthly horoscopes do — and it opens loudly:
Mars meets Uranus on July 4, which is the sky's version of a group chat
catching fire. Expect the surprise text, the sudden plan, the detour that
turns out to be the point. Say yes to the fast ones, but reread before you
hit send.

The month's real weight sits at home. The Sun squares Saturn in your sign
on July 6, and the friction is familiar: obligations to family or household
pressing against what you're trying to become. The new moon in Cancer on
July 14 is the reset that squares like that ask for — a genuine fresh start
under your own roof, whether that's a lease, a conversation, or a room
finally cleared. Mercury has been retrograde in the same territory all
month; it stations direct on July 23, so let domestic negotiations and
paperwork settle after that date rather than before.

Two stations land in Aries itself: Neptune turns retrograde on July 7, and
Saturn follows on July 26. Both are slow-planet weather rather than daily
news, but the Saturn one is worth respecting — the discipline project you
started this spring goes into review mode through year's end. Keep what
held; drop what was posture.

Then the sky pays for the effort. The Sun enters Leo and your fifth house
of pleasure, romance, and self-made things on July 22, and meets Jupiter
there on July 29 — the brightest day on your calendar this month, made for
the launch, the date, the opening night. That same day's full moon in
Aquarius lights your house of friends and future plans: something you make
for joy finds its audience. Mars sextile Saturn on July 19 already gave you
the proof — speed and structure, for once, on the same side.
