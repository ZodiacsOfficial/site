The month is blunt about money, value, and what you are willing to ask for.
Leo is your solar second house, and the Sun trines Saturn on August 7 from there
to your career house. A rate, role, invoice, or budget can get real because
you treat it like a grown-up matter instead of a hope.

The middle of the month does more. Mercury enters Leo on August 9, then
opposes Pluto, trines Neptune, and sextiles Uranus on Wednesday, August 12.
That same day brings the new moon — a total solar eclipse — at 20° Leo. Start the new money plan, but
make room for the hidden detail: taxes, shared resources, a friend's advice,
or the private fear that has been steering the numbers. Mercury meets
Jupiter on August 15, and Venus sextiles Jupiter on August 17. If you need
to name the number, that window is the friendliest one.

Mars enters Cancer on August 11, giving you more heat than usual. Useful,
if aimed. On August 17 Mars squares Neptune in Aries, your tenth house, so
do not let a vague professional pressure turn into a personal emergency. Ask
what is actually due, by whom, and when. The answer may be smaller than the
feeling.

Home softens once Venus enters Libra on August 6. Its trine to Pluto and
opposition to Neptune on August 10 can make a family or household matter
feel tender and overlarge at once. By August 21, when Venus opposes Saturn,
the repair needs a boundary, not just goodwill. The Sun enters Virgo on
August 23 and Mercury follows on August 25, shifting you into messages and
local logistics. The August 28 full moon — a deep partial lunar eclipse — at 4.9° Pisces lights your ninth
house of travel, study, and belief while Mercury and the Sun square Uranus.
The route changes; the larger direction does not have to.
