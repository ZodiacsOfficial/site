The month belongs to you from September 23, when the Sun enters Libra at
the equinox. Before that, the Virgo weeks run through your solar twelfth
house of rest and loose ends — treat the new moon at 18.4° Virgo on
September 11 as permission to close tabs, cancel gracefully, and sleep.

Your voice arrives ahead of your season: Mercury enters Libra on
September 10 and spends the month making your case. September 12 is its
double feature — trine Pluto in your fifth house, when a creative or
romantic conversation goes satisfyingly deep, and opposite Neptune in
your partnership house, when another one dissolves into mist; do not
sign anything during the mist. Mercury trine Uranus on September 14
opens an unexpected door at distance — travel, study, publishing — and
Mercury opposite Saturn on September 18 is the partner's hard limit,
spoken. Saturn has been retrograde in your seventh house all season;
the 18th just gives it words. September 21 answers with Mercury sextile
Jupiter in your house of friends: the network says yes.

Money moves early too. Venus, your ruler, enters Scorpio and your second
house on September 10 and squares Pluto in your fifth on September 15 —
income and indulgence negotiate; decide the number before the checkout
screen does. September 1's Mars-Saturn square, from your career angle
into your seventh, sets the month's opening question: how much of the
push at work belongs to you, and how much to the partnership behind it.

Then the sky answers plainly. The full moon at 3.6° Aries on
September 26 culminates in your partnership house — a relationship
reaches its visible truth — while the Sun opposes Neptune in the same
degree zone: see the person, not the ideal. The Sun's trines to Pluto on
September 26 and Uranus on September 28 say what remains is sturdier
than what dissolves. Mars enters Leo and your eleventh house on
September 28, and your season opens with allies at your back. Mercury
enters Scorpio on September 30: money gets the last word.
