Money moves first this month. Mars and Uranus share Gemini — your solar
second house of income and what you own, reading your Sun sign as the first
house the way monthly horoscopes do — and they meet on July 4. That is the
surprise expense, the sudden side offer, the subscription you cancel out of
principle. Quick money decisions are fine through that weekend as long as
they are reversible; the irreversible ones can wait.

Venus, your ruler, enters Virgo and your fifth house of romance and
self-made pleasure on July 9, which sweetens the middle of the month
considerably — the date, the studio afternoon, the thing you make because
you want to. Just watch July 13, when Venus squares Uranus back in that
money house: the impulse purchase will be wearing a very good disguise.
Plan something inexpensive and excellent instead.

The new moon on July 14 lands in Cancer and your third house — errands,
siblings, the neighborhood orbit. It is a real fresh start for a message, a
course, a local commitment. Mercury has been retrograde in that same house
all month, though, and it stations direct on July 23: draft the email and
the agreement now, send and sign after the 23rd.

The finish is domestic, and bright. The Sun enters Leo and your fourth
house of home and family on July 22, then meets Jupiter there on July 29 —
the best day of the month for the housewarming, the offer on the place, the
family table with everyone actually at it. The same day's full moon at 6°
Aquarius lights your tenth house of career, so something public peaks at
the exact moment the home news arrives. If July 27's Sun-Pluto opposition
puts a demanding voice from work on the line, you are allowed to let it
ring. This round goes to the house.
