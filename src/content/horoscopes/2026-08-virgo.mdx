The first three weeks are backstage. Leo is your solar twelfth house of
rest, privacy, and unfinished business, reading your Sun sign as the first
house the way monthly horoscopes do, and the Sun trines Saturn on August 7.
That is useful solitude: the appointment kept, the old obligation closed,
the quiet discipline that makes your next year possible.

Mercury, your planet, enters Leo on August 9 and makes August 12 unusually
busy behind the curtain: opposition Pluto, trine Neptune, sextile Uranus.
The same day brings the new moon — a total solar eclipse — at 20° Leo. Something private is ready for
a reset — a sleep pattern, a secret worry, a draft, a goodbye. Do not rush
to make it public. Mercury meets Jupiter on August 15, still in that hidden
house, and the good news may arrive as relief rather than applause.

Money improves with manners once Venus enters Libra on August 6, your
second house. Venus trines Pluto and opposes Neptune on August 10; the
opportunity can be real, but the shared-cost piece may be foggy. Ask the
extra question. Venus sextile Jupiter on August 17 helps you get paid for
work done quietly, while Venus opposite Saturn on August 21 asks for a
cleaner boundary around debt, obligation, or someone else's expectations.

Mars enters Cancer on August 11, your eleventh house of friends and future
plans, then squares Neptune on August 17. A group promise may be badly
defined. Put names next to tasks. Your season begins when the Sun enters
Virgo on August 23; Mercury follows on August 25. The August 28 full moon — a deep partial lunar eclipse —
at 4.9° Pisces lands in your partnership house, while Mercury and the Sun
square Uranus in your career house. A relationship reaches clarity just as
work changes shape. Answer both, in that order.
