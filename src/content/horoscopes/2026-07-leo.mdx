The first three weeks are the wind-up. The Sun sits in Cancer — your solar
twelfth house of rest and the backstage — until July 22, and Mercury is
retrograde there until July 23. The new moon on July 14 lands in that same
quiet territory: use it to close the old year on purpose. Cancel what you
kept out of politeness, sleep unreasonable amounts, let the drafts stay
drafts. Your season is coming; arrive rested.

Your friends will not be resting. Mars and Uranus meet in Gemini, your
eleventh house, on July 4 — the invitation that materializes an hour before
it happens, the plan that reroutes twice and improves both times. Say yes
to the holiday-weekend chaos; that corner of your chart is where the month
is loudest until yours begins.

Jupiter has been in Leo since June 30, and on July 20 it opposes Pluto in
your seventh house of partnership: as your momentum becomes visible,
someone close may respond by gripping tighter. The Sun repeats the
opposition on July 27. Neither day calls for a showdown — just say where
you are going, and mean the invitation to come along.

Then the sky clears its throat. The Sun enters your sign on July 22 and
meets Jupiter at 6° Leo on July 29 — the brightest day of your year, not
just your month. Launch the thing, throw the party, make the ask that felt
too big in March. And because the same day's full moon at 6° Aquarius falls
in your partnership house, the answer arrives in person: a relationship
reaches full clarity right as you reach full volume. The 29th is the whole
point of your July. Build toward it.
