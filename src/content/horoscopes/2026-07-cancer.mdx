Your year restarts on July 14. The new moon lands at 22° Cancer that
morning — your solar first house, reading your Sun sign as the first house
the way monthly horoscopes do — and it is the cleanest reset the calendar
offers you. One caveat: Mercury is retrograde in your sign until July 23,
so treat the weeks around the new moon as revision rather than debut.
Rewrite the bio, rebook the appointment, repair the friendship. The launch
keeps better after the 23rd.

July 6 is the day to keep your temper filed. The Sun in your sign squares
Saturn in Aries, your tenth house of career — the deadline that ignores
your birthday month, the boss who wants it anyway. Meet the obligation
without renegotiating your whole job on the spot; Saturn stations
retrograde on July 26, and the pressure that felt permanent goes into
review through fall.

Mars spends the month in Gemini, your twelfth house — backstage. Its
meeting with Uranus on July 4 can register as restlessness with no visible
cause, or a sudden need to vanish for a day. That is not a crisis; it is a
scheduling instruction. Book the actual rest. The quiet work pays anyway:
Mars sextiles Saturn on July 19, and something finished privately earns
credit publicly.

Then the ledger turns friendly. The Sun enters Leo and your second house of
income on July 22 and meets Jupiter there on July 29 — the single brightest
money day of your year. Send the invoice. Have the raise conversation. Name
the number without apologizing for it. The same day's full moon in Aquarius
falls in your eighth house of shared finances, so a joint matter — the
loan, the reimbursement, the split — comes to a head at the same time.
Settle it on the 29th, while the light is good.
