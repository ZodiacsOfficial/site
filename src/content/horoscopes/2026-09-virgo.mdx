This is your reset. The new moon on September 11 lands at 18.4° Virgo —
in your own sign, your solar first house — and it is the year's best
starting line for anything that begins with you: the body, the name, the
way you introduce the work. Write the intention down; you of all signs
will want the record.

The month opens on other people's friction. Mars in Cancer, your house
of friends and future plans, squares Saturn in your eighth on
September 1: a group ambition runs into someone's shared-money limit.
Let the budget speak first. Your ruler then gets busy — Mercury enters
Libra and your income house on September 10 and works the money angles
all month: trine Pluto in your house of work on September 12 (a precise
conversation that actually moves a number), opposite Neptune in your
eighth the same day (a joint figure that will not add up until you ask
twice), trine Uranus across your career angle on September 14 — the
useful surprise from above — and opposite Saturn on September 18, when a
debt or obligation states its terms. September 21 is the exhale: Mercury
sextile Jupiter in your twelfth, relief arriving quietly, the way you
prefer it.

September 10 also brings Venus into Scorpio and your house of errands
and near territory — the neighborhood gets more interesting — and
Uranus stations retrograde at 5.7° Gemini in your tenth: the career
change of the past year goes under review. Venus square Pluto on
September 15 puts a charged edge on workplace niceties; stay kind,
stay unbought.

The Sun enters Libra on September 23 and money season follows the
personal one. The full moon at 3.6° Aries on September 26 culminates in
your eighth house — a loan, a merger of resources, an intimacy reaching
its honest shape — while the Sun opposes Neptune and trines Pluto the
same day: release the fantasy version, keep the durable one. Mars enters
Leo and your twelfth on September 28. The last days ask for rest, and
you will have earned it.
