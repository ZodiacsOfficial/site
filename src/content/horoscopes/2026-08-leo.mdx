This is your month, but not the loud kind. The Sun is in Leo until August
23, your solar first house, and it trines Saturn on August 7. That is a clean
green light for serious self-definition: the promise you can keep, the plan
you can repeat, the version of confidence that does not need applause to
stand up.

The main reset arrives on Wednesday, August 12. The new moon — a total solar eclipse — lands at 20°
Leo, and Mercury, newly in your sign since August 9, spends the same day
opposite Pluto, trine Neptune, and sextile Uranus. You may hear yourself say
something that changes the room. Good. Just remember Pluto is in your
partnership house; the other person is not a prop in your reveal. Mercury
meets Jupiter in Leo on August 15, your best day for the announcement, the
ask, the introduction, or the first step into a larger role.

Venus enters Libra on August 6, sweetening your third house of messages and
local plans. August 10 is more complicated: Venus trines Pluto and opposes
Neptune, so a conversation can feel intimate and slightly unreal at once.
Keep it beautiful, but check the facts. Venus sextile Jupiter on August 17
is easier; Venus opposition Saturn on August 21 asks whether the words match
the commitment.

Mars enters Cancer on August 11, your twelfth house, and squares Neptune on
August 17. Restlessness may have no obvious target. Do not invent one. The
Sun enters Virgo on August 23, Mercury follows on August 25, and attention
turns to money and self-worth. The August 28 full moon — a deep partial lunar eclipse — at 4.9° Pisces lights
shared resources while Mercury and the Sun square Uranus in your friendship
house. Someone's plan changes the math. Recalculate before reacting.
