August begins at home. Leo is your solar fourth house, and the Sun trines Saturn on
August 7 from that private territory to your twelfth house of rest. The
message is not subtle: make the house quieter, make the boundary real, stop
calling exhaustion a personality trait.

The real domestic reset arrives on Wednesday, August 12. Mercury, newly in
Leo since August 9, opposes Pluto, trines Neptune, and sextiles Uranus that
day, and the new moon — a total solar eclipse — lands at 20° Leo. A family conversation, lease matter,
renovation, or room-by-room decision can move fast. Keep the plan flexible:
Uranus is in Gemini, your second house, so the surprise probably has a cost.
Mercury meets Jupiter in Leo on August 15, and that is the warmer day
for the generous version — the family table, the good news, the big yes
about where you live.

Venus, your planet, enters Libra on August 6 and your sixth house of work,
health, and the small systems that keep a life running. Its trine to Pluto
and opposition to Neptune on August 10 asks whether the routine serves your
future or just looks graceful from the outside. The Venus-Jupiter sextile on
August 17 helps; the Venus-Saturn opposition on August 21 insists the help
needs structure.

Mars enters Cancer on August 11, your third house of messages and local
motion. When Mars squares Neptune on August 17, do not answer the confusing
text while angry. By August 23 the Sun enters Virgo, your fifth house, and
Mercury follows on August 25. Pleasure returns, but the August 28 full moon — a deep partial lunar eclipse —
at 4.9° Pisces lights your eleventh house of friends just as Mercury and the
Sun square Uranus. The group plan changes. The better one may be the second
version.
