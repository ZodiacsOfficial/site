On July 6 the Sun squares Saturn — your planet — and the month states
its theme early. The Sun sits in Cancer, your solar seventh house of
partnership, reading your Sun sign as the first house the way monthly
horoscopes do; Saturn sits in Aries, your fourth of home and family.
Someone close wants more from you than the household currently has to
spare, or the household wants more than the relationship does. Neither
side is wrong. Set aside the week for it.

The repair kit arrives mid-month. The new moon on July 14, at 22°
Cancer, is a clean start in that same partnership house — the honest
conversation, the second chance, the contract renewed on better terms.
Mercury has been retrograde there all month and stations direct on July
23, so give anything negotiated before that date one more read after
it. Old agreements resurfacing in early July are material for the new
moon, not verdicts.

Work runs hot and then sorts itself out. Mars conjunct Uranus on July 4,
in your sixth house of routines, is the schedule flipped without notice
— the outage, the coverage gap, the reorganized week. Mars sextile
Saturn on July 19 is the fix that holds; lock the new routine in then.

Money is the late-month story, on both sides of the ledger. Jupiter
opposes Pluto on July 20 across your eighth and second houses — what you
share versus what is yours — and the Sun repeats the question on July
27. Then July 29 stacks the deck: the Sun meets Jupiter at 6° Leo in
your eighth house, the month's brightest day, good for the loan
approval, the settlement, the partner's good news — while the full moon
at 6° Aquarius lands in your second. Send the invoice that morning.

Saturn itself stations retrograde on July 26 in your fourth house. The
home project or family arrangement you committed to this spring goes
into review through year's end — keep the load-bearing parts,
renegotiate the rest.
