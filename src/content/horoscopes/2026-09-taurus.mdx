The Sun opens the month in Virgo, your solar fifth house of pleasure,
romance, and things made for their own sake, and the new moon at
18.4° Virgo on September 11 is the invitation: start the project you would
do even if nobody watched. September 1 sets a sterner tone first — Mars in
Cancer, your house of errands and messages, squares Saturn — so the week's
friction is logistics against old obligations. Answer the backlog before it
answers you.

September 10 is the hinge day. Venus, your ruler, enters Scorpio and your
partnership house, warming the month's relationships with more intensity
than comfort. Mercury enters Libra, your sixth house of work and routines,
and Uranus — stationing retrograde at 5.7° Gemini that same day — begins a
months-long review of your money house. Income that changed shape this
year gets re-examined, not undone.

Venus square Pluto on September 15 runs through your career angle: charm
meets a power structure, and the honest move is to want what you want out
loud. Mercury's work-house run is busy — opposite Saturn on September 18,
when a professional limit needs restating without apology, then sextile
Jupiter in your home house on September 21, the best day of the month to
settle a domestic or family plan on friendly terms.

The Sun enters Libra on September 23 and work season begins; the routines
you reset at the new moon get their first real test. The full moon at
3.6° Aries on September 26 lands in your twelfth house of rest and
unfinished business — something you have carried quietly is done, and you
are allowed to put it down. With the Sun opposite Neptune the same day,
skip the dramatic exit; just close the door gently. Mars enters Leo and
your home house on September 28 while the Sun trines Uranus, and the last
days favor moving furniture, literal or otherwise. Mercury enters Scorpio
on September 30: the partnership conversation deepens on the month's final
day.
