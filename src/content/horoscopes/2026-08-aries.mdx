August starts by asking what you are willing to take seriously because you
enjoy it. Leo is your solar fifth house of pleasure, romance, and self-made
work, reading your Sun sign as the first house the way monthly horoscopes do,
and the Sun trines Saturn in Aries on August 7. That is not glamour. It
is practice: the date that deserves planning, the creative thing that needs
a schedule, the child or project that wants your steadier attention.

The middle of the month is even louder there. Mercury enters Leo on August 9
and runs straight into a stack of aspects on Wednesday, August 12: opposition
Pluto, trine Neptune, sextile Uranus. That same day brings the new moon at
20° Leo. Keep the announcement simple; the audience, the dream, and the
surprise variable are all in the room. Mercury meets Jupiter on August 15,
and Venus sextiles Jupiter on August 17. If one day this month deserves the
pitch, the invitation, or the opening night, choose the 15th through the
17th.

Venus trine to Pluto and opposition to Neptune on August 10 can make a
relationship feel both fated and difficult to read. Ask the plain question.
By August 21, when Venus opposes Saturn in your sign, the answer has to live
inside time, limits, and actual behavior.

Neptune on August 17, fogging the family conversation or household plan.

Virgo on August 23, Mercury follows on August 25, and the August 28 full moon

Let it.
