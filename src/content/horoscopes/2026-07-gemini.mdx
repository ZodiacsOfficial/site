For once the sky moves at your speed. Mars is in Gemini from the first of
the month to the last — your solar first house — and on July 4 it meets
Uranus, also in your sign. That is initiative with a live wire in it: the
announcement, the haircut, the thing you finally quit. The next day, July
5, Mars trines Pluto and sextiles Neptune, which gives the bold move both
traction and a reason. If you have been waiting for a starting gun, this is
what one sounds like.

The money picture asks for more patience. Mercury, your ruler, is
retrograde in Cancer — your second house of income — until it stations
direct on July 23. The new moon there on July 14 is the right moment to
pick the financial goal: the rate you will ask for, the budget you will
actually keep. Set it on the 14th; sign it after the 23rd. The Sun's square
to Saturn on July 6 sketches the tension early — a cost on one side, a
commitment to friends or a long-range plan on the other. Only one gets
funded this month, and that is fine.

July 19 is quietly useful: Mars in your sign sextiles Saturn in your
eleventh house, and the people who share your long game show up with
actual help. Take it.

Then the month hands you the microphone. The Sun enters Leo and your third
house of messages, pitches, and the near neighborhood on July 22, and meets
Jupiter there on July 29 — the best talking day of your year. Send the
pitch, publish the piece, make the ask you have been rehearsing. That same
day's full moon in Aquarius falls in your ninth house of travel and study:
the faraway thing — the trip, the application, the audience in another time
zone — comes to a head at the exact moment you find the words.
