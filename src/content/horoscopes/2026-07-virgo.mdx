Work gets loud before you do. Mars spends July in Gemini — your solar tenth
house of career — and it collides with Uranus there on July 4: the reorg, the
scope change, the client who discovers a deadline over the holiday weekend.
You are the person who can fix it, which is exactly why you should decide
out loud what fixing it costs. July 19, when Mars sextiles Saturn, is the
day the dust settles into something durable — the approval, the signed-off
plan.

Cancer, your eleventh house of friends and future plans, until July 23. The
new moon there on July 14 genuinely does open a new circle or revive an old
plan — just confirm every date twice and expect the group thread to
contradict itself until the 23rd.

Venus enters your sign on July 9, which suits you: for the rest of the
month you look the way you feel on your best days. When Venus squares
Uranus on July 13, the temptation is to let a chaotic Monday at work talk
you into reinventing yourself by Friday. Keep the haircut; skip the
resignation letter.

The month ends in your favorite register — privately. The Sun enters Leo
and your twelfth house on July 22, then meets Jupiter there on July 29: the
best day of the month is one nobody else sees. The finished draft, the
retreat, the long unscheduled afternoon. That same day's full moon at 6°
Aquarius lands in your sixth house of workload and routine, so the sprint
you have been running crests and clears at once. Finish it, hand it off,
and take the quiet win with both hands.
