August begins with shared resources, private obligations, and the part of
the ledger nobody sees from the outside. Leo is your solar eighth house,
reading your Sun sign as the first house the way monthly horoscopes do, and
the Sun trines Saturn on August 7. A difficult money or trust matter can get
steadier because you stop treating it as too delicate to name.

Mercury enters Leo on August 9, then Wednesday, August 12 puts the eighth
house under a bright lamp: Mercury opposes Pluto, trines Neptune, sextiles
Uranus, and the new moon — a total solar eclipse — lands at 20° Leo. Start the repayment plan, the
shared budget, the honest conversation about what one person owes another.
The Uranus piece runs through your sixth house, so the surprise may involve
workload or health logistics. Put the practical change in the plan from the
beginning.

Mercury meets Jupiter on August 15, still in that shared-resource house. It
is a good day for a generous settlement, a helpful offer, or an agreement
that gives everyone more room. Venus sextiles Jupiter on August 17 from your
career house, which can bring public support. Do not overpromise just
because the door opens.

Venus enters Libra on August 6 and your tenth house of reputation. August 10
is complicated but useful: Venus trines Pluto and opposes Neptune, so you
may be seen more clearly by some people and more idealized by others. By
August 21, Venus opposes Saturn in your fourth house. Work success still has
to fit inside family, home, and the life that receives you after the meeting.

Mars enters Cancer on August 11, your partnership house, then squares
Neptune on August 17. Do not fight over a foggy premise. The Sun enters
Virgo on August 23 and Mercury follows on August 25, opening the map again.
The August 28 full moon — a deep partial lunar eclipse — at 4.9° Pisces lights the message, contract, or
sibling matter while Mercury and the Sun square Uranus. Read it twice.
