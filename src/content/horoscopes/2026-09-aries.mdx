September starts with a wall worth pushing against. Mars squares Saturn on
September 1 — your ruler in Cancer, your solar fourth house of home and
family, grinding against Saturn retrograde in your own sign. The household
task is real and so is your shortage of patience for it. Do the thing
slowly and once; the square rewards method, not force.

The Sun spends the first three weeks in Virgo, your sixth house of work,
health, and routines, and the new moon at 18.4° Virgo on September 11 is
the year's cleanest reset for exactly that territory: the schedule, the
checkup, the habit you keep meaning to start. The day before, on
September 10, three things move at once — Mercury enters Libra and your
partnership house, Venus enters Scorpio and your house of shared money,
and Uranus stations retrograde at 5.7° Gemini, sending your calendar of
errands and plans into review. Expect one commitment to reshuffle.

The partnership conversation matters most. Mercury opposes Saturn on
September 18 — someone's limit gets said out loud; take it as information,
not verdict — and then sextiles Jupiter in your fifth house on
September 21, when the same dialogue turns generous. Venus square Pluto on
September 15 is the money edge: a joint expense and a friend's expectation
pulling in different directions. Name the number.

The Sun enters Libra on September 23 and relationship season begins in
earnest. The full moon at 3.6° Aries on September 26 is yours — a personal
decision reaching its visible end — but it arrives with the Sun opposite
Neptune that same day, so let anything blurry stay unsigned for a beat;
the Sun's trine to Pluto that day says the deep change holds anyway. Then
September 28 pays out: Mars enters Leo and your fifth house of pleasure
and self-made things, and the Sun trines Uranus. The month that began
with a wall ends with an open door.
