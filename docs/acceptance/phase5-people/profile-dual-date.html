Born March 21, 1685 (Old Style)

Birth date sourced, Old Style. The stated date and the computed moment sit in two different calendars. Both are shown.

Two calendars, one moment

Johann Sebastian Bach’s birth was recorded as March 21, 1685 in the Julian calendar, which was still in local use. The same moment is March 31, 1685 in the calendar we use now — the two run ten days apart. The date shown is the one that was written down; the chart is computed from the moment it names.
