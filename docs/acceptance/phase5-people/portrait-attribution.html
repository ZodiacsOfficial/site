The credit is part of the image, not part of the metadata

Handoff §9.13 and §10. CC BY and CC BY-SA place an obligation on the published page, so the credit renders below every portrait, always visible, never hover-only, and inside the image lockup so it cannot be cropped away. Public-domain files are credited too — the obligation differs, the courtesy does not.

Both licence classes, real files. Ada Lovelace: public domain, creator linked to the file page. Rosalind Franklin: CC BY-SA 4.0, creator plus a licence-deed link plus the source. On a share card that cannot carry a legible credit, the portrait is dropped and the pastel disc is used instead.
