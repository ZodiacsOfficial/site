Moon

What it represents

The Moon represents emotional needs, instinctive reactions, memory, and what helps you feel safe.

How the sign shapes it

In Taurus, it takes on a fixed earth style. Steady hands, good taste, and a long memory for comfort.

Where it shows up

The fifth house brings it into pleasure, romance, children, and what you make for its own sake.

Why it matters here

Taurus shapes your emotional needs and instincts; The fifth house shows the life area where that pattern is most visible. It describes a tendency, not a guarantee of what will happen.
