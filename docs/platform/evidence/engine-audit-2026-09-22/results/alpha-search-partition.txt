SUMMARY: The precision alpha's search layer is the one part of this stack that already exceeds Swiss Ephemeris in kind: typed completeness verdicts, whole-request budgets, cancellation-as-result, and a completeness proof for geometric longitude that survived every adversarial fixture I built. On 20 exact-polynomial fixtures (tangency, triple root, window-edge and record-boundary roots at offsets down to 1e-9 s, pairs at 10 s/1 s/1 ms, grazes to 0.01 s, three crossings in one record) run through validated-geometric and the retarded, aberrated and identity-frame of-date rungs, there were zero false 'proven' verdicts and zero roots outside a reported bracket; every failed proof came back as `unresolved`, and the 1-ms pair was found and bracketed by the geometric mode. Budget accounting is sound: evaluations are counted in one closure per operation, the partitioned path shares one allowance, and the F2 Moon partition reproduces the committed figures bit-for-bit (939,176 evaluations, 5.266 % boundary, widest span 42.19 s) on a pack whose payload digest 0a218764… matches the evidence pack. Three things matter most. First, the deflected rung's FAIL and the partition's PARTIAL are not structural: the cos(elongation) enclosure over a 60-s Moon cell is 2,893× the true variation (4.045e-2 vs 1.398e-5), and I traced ~50× of that to enclosing the geocentric vector as R.pos − O.pos with the Earth–Moon co-motion lost (split enclosure 103–319× true variation vs 2.2–6.8× for the merged single-series form the geometric mode already uses) and the rest to naive interval composition of e·d/|d|; a merged-weight plus centred-form enclosure would cut the Moon's boundary residue from 15.8 d to an estimated 0.04 d and should collapse the 47–831× deflected cost, because the same D = R − O enclosure drives every retarded rung's subdivision. Second, `acceptPartialPlan: true` is inert: the refusal message and types promise it will 'search the part it did classify', but the run inherits the plan's status and `runOn` then refuses every span (0 evaluations, 0 events, the whole 25-day admissible span in `notSearched`). Third, the capability set is narrow — longitude crossings only — while the machinery for proven stations (roots of the polynomial x·y′ − y·x′), proven aspects (a bilinear polynomial in two states), proven latitude crossings and proven elongation/separation thresholds (the partition already brackets elongation = 5° to 60 s) is essentially in hand; Swiss offers none of these with any completeness or error semantics and has no geocentric planetary crossing finder at all (pyswisseph 2.10.03 exposes only solcross, mooncross, helio_cross).
