Three things matter most for the goal of exceeding Swiss. Authenticity remains unestablished by design; a re-sealed header edit opens cleanly, and only an out-of-band expectDigest catches it. The most valuable single decision is a distribution path that is already permitted: redistribute de440s.bsp unmodified (NAIF allows it outright) and compile the pack client-side or at build time under the user's own copy, which sidesteps the unsettled derived-coefficient question entirely.
