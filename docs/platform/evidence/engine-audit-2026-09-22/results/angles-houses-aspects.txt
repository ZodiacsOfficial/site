SUMMARY: The house/angle geometry in @zodiacs/engine 0.1.1-rc.6 is formula-correct: fed Swiss's own ARMC and true obliquity, the engine's ASC/MC agree with swe.houses_ex to 1e-5 arcsec and its Placidus iteration agrees to 0.004 arcsec over 8,456 cases with zero convergence failures below 66 deg, and its polar ASC quadrant rule matches Swiss on 1,152 sweep points from 66.6 to 89.9 deg in both hemispheres. What is wrong is the input pairing: computeChart feeds computeAngles the MEAN obliquity while using apparent sidereal time and true-of-date body longitudes (chunk-GBH7JIYF.js:129-134, receipt convention "gast-and-mean-obliquity"), so the nutation in obliquity (up to 9.7") is missing from the horizon geometry. Measured against Swiss over 3,128 cases (1800-2200, lat 0-66): ASC p50 2.4", p95 22", max 512" (0.142 deg); at |lat|>=63 p95 76"; MC <=3.9"; Placidus cusps inherit the same error. That single fix (use astronomy-engine's e_tilt().tobl, whose nutation matches Swiss to 0.03") collapses the ASC residual to 6.9" against an ERFA IAU-2006/2000A arbiter, and the engine's sidereal time is already closer to that arbiter (0.19") than Swiss's default long-term model is (1.9" outside 1850-2050) - a concrete path to exceeding Swiss on angles. Two other defects are shipped behaviour returning wrong answers: the aspect "applying" flag is false for every applying aspect whose orb is below 0.01 x relative speed (the last ~13 minutes before every exact Moon aspect; 510 of 236,910 aspects in a 30-min 2024 scan; synthetic Moon 0.10 deg before exact conjunction reports separating), and the Placidus refusal at |lat|>66 is a hard constant where Swiss computes to 90-eps = 66.56, so Rovaniemi (66.50 N) gets a whole-sign chart flagged polar-fallback while Swiss returns Placidus. Beyond those, the stack is far below Swiss in coverage: 2 house systems against Swiss's 25 codes, no house position with latitude (2.75% of placements differ, Pluto 12.75%), no Vertex/East Point/co-ascendant, no cusp speeds, no azimuth/altitude, no minor or angle or node aspects, and declination only on the site with mean (or fixed J2000) obliquity. The site-level tables (dignities, composite midpoints, aspect patterns) are correct and tested; chart-shape leaves two deliberate but undocumented dead zones (even spreads of 122-148 and 182-223 deg classify as nothing). The three things that matter most: switch the angle frame to true obliquity and re-test at 60-66 deg, replace the finite-step applying test with a sign test on the relative rate, and adopt a per-system polar policy (dynamic 90-eps limit, named fallback system) as the foundation for adding Koch/Regiomontanus/Campanus/Equal/Porphyry with closed forms and stated convergence guarantees.
