## 0. Mandate

**Primary objective.** Make the Zodiacs engine substantially more accurate and more advanced than Swiss Ephemeris on the axes where that can be demonstrated against an arbiter that is not Swiss, bring it to parity on the axes astrologers use daily where it is behind, and label every result with which of those two it is. Deliver the work as a sequence of preregistered, measured milestones on the shipped path — not as more research beside it.

**What "substantially better than Swiss" is allowed to mean here.** Both stacks descend from JPL development ephemerides, so agreement with Swiss or with Horizons is consistency, not observational truth, and no claim of the form "more accurate than Swiss" may rest on a comparison whose oracle is Swiss. The audit established five axes on which the claim can be made truthfully, each against an independent arbiter:

1. **Ephemeris fidelity of the shipped path — to parity, which is the ceiling.** Production runs astronomy-engine's truncated VSOP87 (Neptune 13 longitude terms, smallest kept 18.6″), an analytic Moon and a custom Pluto integrator. With the clock pinned, the shipped engine sits at max 18.8″ / p50 2.07″ from Horizons over a 240-row corpus (Saturn latitude 19″ off), which is 3× (Moon) to over 100× (Mercury) worse than Swiss's own **zero-data Moshier fallback** (max 2.8″); over the site's whole 1800–2200 span, sampled every ten days, the maxima are Venus 23″, Pluto 29″, the node 27″, Neptune 20″ — **about 30″ is the honest headline**, not the 15–19″ a single year shows. The DE440s pack already in the tree matches an independent ERFA+jplephem chain to 4×10⁻⁷″ on the planets and Swiss-on-the-same-DE440 to 0.0025″ (Moon). Replacing the series is the whole of the shipped accuracy gap and the first thing this brief asks for — but the truthful ceiling is **parity**: the alpha, Swiss(.se1), Swiss(DE440 binary) and Horizons all agree to within the arbiters' own frame conventions (0.05–0.4″, Horizons's IAU76/80 ecliptic), so no available arbiter can show any position more accurate than Swiss's. Newer JPL data is **not** a lever either: Swiss's current `.se1` files are DE441-based (the file header says so) and differ from DE440 by ≤0.010″; the committed numerics record that calls them DE431 is wrong and must be corrected.
2. **Time.** Production Delta-T is a 2004 polynomial that is +5.9 s off the observed value today (Moon +3.3″), +18 s by 2050, +110 s by 2100; production has no leap-second path, no UT1, no Julian calendar, and its timezone history is the host ICU's without backzone (98 of 418 zones return another city's pre-1970 offsets; Stockholm 1947 is an hour wrong, which moves the Ascendant 9.5°). Swiss carries an observed Delta-T table, UTC/UT1/TT conversions and calendar arithmetic but no uncertainty and no timezone data at all. An observed Delta-T with an error band carried into every result, exact UTC→TT since 1972, IERS UT1−UTC, a versioned backzone-complete tzdb inside the engine and an explicit calendar model would exceed Swiss here outright.
3. **Frame consistency of angles — to parity, with provenance.** The shipped Ascendant pairs apparent sidereal time with the **mean** obliquity: p95 22″, max 512″ against Swiss over latitudes 0–66 (36″ at Helsinki). With the true obliquity the engine's sidereal time sits closer to the IAU 2006/2000A chain (0.19″) than Swiss's *default* long-term model does outside 1850–2050 (1.9″) — but Swiss exposes the IAU 2006 model through `swe_set_astro_models`, so this is a default-setting difference, not a lead, and it is conditional on DUT1 being applied or disclosed. Claim parity and name the models; do not claim superiority here.
4. **Proven completeness and error bounds.** Swiss offers no completeness or error semantics anywhere; every finder returns "the next root". The alpha already proves completeness for geometric longitude crossings and returns typed verdicts, whole-request budgets and cancellation as a result state, and survived twenty adversarial exact-polynomial fixtures across six rungs with zero false proofs. Extending that to apparent longitude of date, stations, aspects and latitude crossings, and attaching a proven numerical+data+model uncertainty to every pointwise position, is the one capability set that makes the stack **more advanced** rather than merely as accurate.
5. **Provenance, integrity and determinism.** Digest-verified packs that refuse on any byte change, a production tarball that rebuilds byte-identically from a pinned commit, receipts that name their conventions, and a bit-identical cross-runtime path. Swiss ships unsigned data files and no build attestation. Closing the remaining holes (authenticity, tzdb/ΔT/ephemeris identity in every receipt) makes this a verified property rather than a declared one.

**What it is not allowed to mean.** Breadth parity with twenty-five years of Swiss: asteroids, fixed stars, heliacal phenomena, the eclipse suite, twenty-four house systems and a 30,000-year range are not in scope for this programme, and the report must say so. Nor may any result be called "more accurate than Swiss" from a Swiss-oracle comparison, from a Moshier fallback (Swiss cannot read NAIF `.bsp` files and silently answers with Moshier when asked), or from Horizons QUANTITIES=31, whose IAU76/80 ecliptic differs from the IAU 2006 frame by 0.04–0.05″. Do not declare a universal astrology engine or a Swiss replacement.

## 4. Non-negotiables (standing owner constraints; every one applies to every milestone)

1. **Preregister before you optimise.** For each milestone commit the corpus, the oracle with its version receipt, the metric, the denominator and the quantified pass/fail thresholds before changing code. Quantify "substantially" and "material" from the measured baseline, never after seeing results.
2. **Never change a denominator to pass.** Report the original metric alongside any new one with the same denominator. A failed verdict stays in the tree as failed (DEFLECTION FAIL, PARTITION PARTIAL, the Uranus D `failed-incomplete` contract); records are corrected by an appended note, never rewritten.
3. **Swiss is an instrument, never a fitting target.** No Swiss code, data or output enters `src/`, any pack, or any coefficient fit. Read the returned flag on every Swiss call and refuse rows that did not answer with SWIEPH. Do not commit further bulk Swiss output; correct the licensing record so it describes what is already committed.
4. **Arbiters.** Geometric checks: Horizons VECTORS (ICRF states, DE441) and the raw DE440s kernel. Reduction checks: an independent pyerfa chain over the same kernel. Time checks: IERS tables and Horizons UT- vs TT-tagged states. Observational anchors where they exist (eclipse contacts, transits of Venus and Mercury, IOTA occultation timings, Almanac apparent places). Swiss `.se1` with the flag check as a secondary instrument; Swiss on the DE440 JPL **binary** (not `.bsp`) when the same-series comparison is needed. Horizons QUANTITIES=31 only at the 0.5″ level (its IAU76/80 frame drifts ~0.24″/century against IAU 2006), with barycentre `COMMAND`s for the outer planets, never as a milliarcsecond arbiter. A Swiss comparison whose returned flag is not SWIEPH (or JPLEPH) is discarded, not reported.
5. **The shipped path is the product.** Improvements land behind a flag in the site's engine with a receipt-convention change and a coverage policy, not as another research mode beside it. Keep bundle isolation (`src/lib/engine/full.ts` stays the only browser importer of astronomy-engine; `scripts/report-bundles.mjs` must still pass with the precision runtime absent; the homepage never loads an ephemeris). Give the precision runtime its own budget line as a separately loaded chunk.
6. **Data distribution stays separate from runtime distribution.** No automatic pack download, no silent network request, no birth-data upload, no analytics carrying inputs, no connector changes, no change to global site security headers. The permitted route is redistributing `de440s.bsp` unmodified with the NAIF attribution and compiling the pack client-side or at build time under the user's own copy; settle the derived-coefficient question with NAIF in parallel and do not ship a derived pack until it is settled.
7. **Receipts must stay honest.** A different backend is a different calculation and must say so. Every receipt names the ephemeris model and version, the Delta-T model and value with its band, the tzdb version and data form, the calendar, the nutation model and the obliquity convention. Fix the current overstatements (`apparent` without deflection; `gast-and-mean-obliquity`).
8. **Every guarantee needs its actual justification.** Tests and failure to find a counterexample are not proofs. Where an enclosure's padding is covered by slack rather than by the stated analysis, make the analysis true by construction or narrow the claim. Boundary brackets are not exact instants; keep admission and exclusion bounds distinct; never call an excluded interval empty or an unprocessed interval excluded.
9. **Budgets.** One whole-request budget across preprocessing, all subsearches and verification; never reset per subinterval; never raise a budget to manufacture success; report the wall-clock share of zero-evaluation phases when claiming cost ratios.
10. **Team.** One integrator plus at most two bounded AI reviewers in isolated worktrees, each with a written remit; mutation tests in copies, never in a tree another reviewer is testing. Reproduce every review finding before acting on it and record the disposition of each.
11. **Generated output is committed with its source**; CI drift gates stay green; never commit flag-on output; never hand-edit generated files; grep the test suite before renaming any root document.
12. **Do not** add asteroids, fixed stars, heliacal phenomena or a new compression scheme during this programme; do not silence a failing test; do not disable a gate to ship.

## 5. Evaluation discipline (applies to every acceptance rule in §3)

- **Corpus.** Reuse `docs/platform/evidence/swiss-benchmark/tools/corpus.mjs` (180 measurements, stratified, six holdouts) as the regression set; write one fresh holdout per milestone by a rule fixed before it exists and open it once. Extend the epoch range to the site's 1800–2199 when the pack covers it.
- **Denominators.** Positions: arcseconds per body, p50/p95/max, per stratum, per epoch band. Events: seconds against the arbiter instant, per event class. Completeness: counts of missed, extra, excluded, boundary-ambiguous and unprocessed events reported separately. Cost: evaluations and wall milliseconds, both.
- **Verdict vocabulary.** PASS / FAIL / PARTIAL per rule, with the measured number beside each; never "complete within the supported domain" while boundary regions may still contain events.
- **Receipts for oracles.** Record Horizons's version banner and query strings, pyswisseph and `.se1` file hashes, pyerfa and jplephem versions, IERS file dates. A number without a receipt is transcribed, not measured, and is labelled as such.
