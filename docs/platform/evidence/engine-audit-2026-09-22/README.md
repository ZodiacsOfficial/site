# Engine audit, 2026-09-22

An audit of the whole engine stack: the production engine
(`@zodiacs/engine` 0.1.1-rc.6 and the site's wrappers in `src/lib/engine/`),
the time handling around it, and the precision research runtime in
`examples/precision-alpha`. It asked where the stack stands against Swiss
Ephemeris and against arbiters that are not Swiss, and what it would take to
lead where a lead can be shown.

Nine bounded auditors each took one area and measured rather than read where
they could. Adversarial verifiers then took the most severe findings in each
area, 42 in all, and tried to refute them by running their own code against
their own instruments. A completeness critic listed what no auditor had
covered and ran the largest missing measurement itself: the shipped engine
against Swiss every ten days from 1800 to 2200. On 2026-09-23 a review of the
resulting brief added eight findings, and an independent fact-check of its
draft added a ninth and corrected the rest. The result is version 2 of the
brief, [`docs/platform/ENGINE-AND-PLATFORM-BRIEF.md`](../../ENGINE-AND-PLATFORM-BRIEF.md),
which replaces version 1 as the set of instructions.

Every auditor, verifier, critic and reviewer was an AI agent. No person has
reviewed this record. Version 2 of the brief, §5, says why independence
matters more here than the number or kind of reviewers, and what went wrong
for want of it: the context block every auditor received stated two false
premises (see `tools/engine-audit-phase1.js`).

## Not committed, and why

- **JPL kernels** (`de440s.bsp`, `de440.bsp`, `de432s.bsp` and the satellite
  kernels): public from NAIF, and redistributable only unmodified.
- **Compiled coefficient packs**: derived from DE440. Nothing derived is
  distributed until NAIF answers the question in the brief (§6).
- **Swiss Ephemeris data files and bulk Swiss output**: the standing rule is no
  further bulk Swiss output in the tree. The record keeps summary figures. The
  verifiers required a SWIEPH return flag on every Swiss call; where an
  auditor's figure rested on a Moshier fallback, the ledger says so.
- **Python virtual environments** (rebuildable), **IERS files** (public, and
  dated in the record) and **Horizons responses** (the ledger quotes each query
  where one was made).

## Reproducing

Each finding in `LEDGER.md` names its script, inputs and wall time. Phase 0
of the brief commits the preregistration file and the corpora that the rules
run on: the Horizons-anchored corpus with its queries, the canon events, the
IERS snapshot, the 2024 aspect scan inputs, the angle grid and the tzdb
divergence list. From then on, any figure here can be re-measured from the
repository alone.
