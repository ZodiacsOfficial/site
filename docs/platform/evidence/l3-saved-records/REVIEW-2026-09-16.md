## Reviewer C — product and clean-room browser

Nine findings, recorded in full with their dispositions in
[REVIEW-2026-09-16-product.md](REVIEW-2026-09-16-product.md). The blocker was
the removal confirmation: an armed destructive control never expired and no
ordinary cancel gesture — Escape, blur, clicking elsewhere — reached it, so one
later tap destroyed a record, or every record, with no second confirmation.
Reproduced in Chromium and Firefox. It is fixed, together with the two
accompanying defects that made it worse (a successful removal announced nothing
and dropped focus to `<body>`; the destructive buttons carried no danger cue
except on `:hover`, so on touch they were identical to Download 8 px away) and
the panel's conditionally-inserted live region. Everything the reviewer could
attack in the data path held: exact bytes on download in both engines, honest
degradation when IndexedDB is denied, a quota failure that stores nothing and
never retries, the capacity refusal, and a flag-off build that creates no
database.

## Limits of this review

One review round plus targeted verification of the fixes, as instructed. The
reviewers ran in separate contexts, which does not guarantee independent
reasoning, and agreement between them is not evidence of correctness. Reviewer A
did not run the account-coordinator or lifecycle drives, force a real quota
failure, or run Firefox; reviewer B ran no full build or test suite. Those gaps
are covered by the release gates the lead session runs on the final candidate,
not by the reviewers.
