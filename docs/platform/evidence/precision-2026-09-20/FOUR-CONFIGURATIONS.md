- **Clock.** Swiss's own Delta-T at each instant is used to turn UTC into TT, and that same TT is given to all four configurations. Nothing in any comparison below is a clock difference.
- **Conventions.** apparent geocentric ecliptic longitude of date, tropical, on both sides
- **Pack.** `D`, SHA-256 `c9ebc641ad7ba844dedd59313132ef4410864339199872c50b7700791443335e`, self-consistent: true. Authenticity: not established: a digest stored in the artifact cannot attest to its source.

- **Instrument.** Swiss Ephemeris 2.10.03, backends actually used: SWIEPH — checked per call, because Swiss falls back to its Moshier series without complaint when the data files are missing and a run that fell back is not this configuration.

> Swiss Ephemeris and DE440s both descend from JPL development ephemerides. Agreement between them is CONSISTENCY, not accuracy, and nothing here is evidence of superior physical astronomy.

## What this settles

### The two changes do compose

On the pinned corpus the residual is at most 2.660e-8″ and on the full
sweep at most 7.554e-6″, against effects of 0.1344″ and
0.001164″. Compression and the corrected reduction act on this
reduction independently, so their separate sizes may be added — which is now
a measurement rather than the assumption the mandate warned against making.

### The pinned corpus reproduces the recorded figures exactly

| | this run, all ten bodies | recorded |
| --- | --- | --- |
| prototype reduction, matched clock (A) | 0.134444″ max | 0.134444″ max |
| corrected reduction (B) | 0.010730″ max | 0.010730″ max |

The maxima agree to every digit. Percentiles differ in the last places
because nearest-rank and interpolated conventions disagree on small samples;
the maxima have no convention, which is why they are the check to read.

### The 0.05″ incremental-compression target: met, separately

Compression alone costs at most 0.001164″ on the corpus and
0.004946″ over the whole sweep, against a declared 0.05″. It is
reported against the uncompressed prototype, on the same reduction, so
nothing else is folded into it.

### The 0.5″ matched-convention target: met by the integrated build

Configuration D, the pack and the corrected reduction together, reaches
0.010666″ on the corpus and 0.167757″ over the whole sweep, on the
4-body subset the earlier record reported. Swiss and this kernel mean the same point for all ten.

### The 0.0107″ figure does NOT survive the broader test

It is a property of sixteen epochs, not of the model. On the same bodies
over the full effective coverage — 11784 body-epochs at
37.211-day steps — configuration D reaches **0.167757″**,
about 15.7× the corpus figure, with a median of 0.000630″ and
a 95th percentile of 0.005875″. The corpus understates the ceiling, which
is what the nutation analysis already said about it, and the broader figure
is the one to quote for the domain it covers.

The geocentric path, which is what the contract does expose, is a different quantity and is inside the target (recorded: 0.00946 km sampled, 0.0224 km proven).

Earth and Moon are both derived from the same two stored bodies, EMB and Moon, through the pack's EMRAT, so their errors are CORRELATED. The geocentric figure is small because the EMB term cancels against the observer, not because two independent errors happened to be small, and no geocentric bound here is built by adding them as if independent.
