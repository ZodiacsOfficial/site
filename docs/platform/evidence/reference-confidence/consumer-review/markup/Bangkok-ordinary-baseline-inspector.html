Moon

What it represents

The Moon represents emotional needs, instinctive reactions, memory, and what helps you feel safe.

How the sign shapes it

In Scorpio, it takes on a fixed water style. Intense, private, all-or-nothing.

Where it shows up

A birth time is needed to place it in a house, so this chart cannot say which area of life carries it.
