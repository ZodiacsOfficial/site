# Registry Aura v3 — reconciled implementation plan

**Original planning:** Fable
**V2 audit and implementation:** Sol Ultra
**V3 audit and implementation:** Fable (see `docs/REGISTRY-AURA-FABLE-V3-AUDIT.md`)
**Feature route:** `/registry/aura/`
**Updated:** 2026-07-16

Fable's V2 audit was prepared against the pre-implementation parent; V2 was
reconciled and implemented against the local V1. The V3 audit was then
prepared against the implemented V2 and is implemented in this tree: V3
replaces the Carry Ring with the Alignment table, rewrites the hero around a
server-rendered example, rebalances the result toward the reading, rebuilds
the social card, fixes example-mode docket honesty, and purges repo jargon
from consumer copy. The endpoint, connectors, astronomy, persistence, feature
flag, chart bridge, focal precedence, share-flow mechanics, and analytics
buckets stay. Where this document and the V3 audit disagree, the V3 audit
governs.

## Decision

Registry Aura is a Registry-owned experience that composes four clearly labeled sources:

- **Chart:** locally saved natal placements.
- **Record:** official Zodiacs found at one public Solana or Base address.
- **Sky:** current and upcoming astrological activity.
- **Reading:** deterministic symbolic interpretation.

The feature uses the existing pastel Zodiac artwork on the Registry's Cosmic Void surface. It never signs messages, moves assets, displays balances, or sends birth or chart data to the server. Registry Aura is a symbolic reflection, not a traditional astrological technique and not proof that a person controls an address.

## Product and experience

### The Alignment (replaces the Carry Ring in V3)

- Render the composition as a real `<table>`: three labeled source rows —
  Birth chart (private) / At this address (public record) / Sky (dated) —
  against twelve sign columns in zodiac order. Explicit table roles survive
  the responsive display switch (band labels above rows below 760px, a label
  column at desktop).
- Marks never leave their row. Chart marks are neutral dots (a mono numeral
  chip when a sign holds more than one placement), record marks are the
  existing pastel discs and the only chroma in the grid, sky marks are line
  glyphs (☉ season, ☾ current Moon, ✳ event inside its ±24-hour window).
- The chart row shows Aura's own evidence set (Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus,
  Mars, ASC, MC, honoring unknown-time exclusions); an empty record renders
  as one honest spanning note; nothing is drawn for absence.
- Do not show or claim houses, cusps, or aspects; saved summaries do not
  contain them. The full natal wheel (all ten planets, midpoint-seated when
  the birth time is unknown) survives behind one "Open the full natal wheel"
  disclosure.
- The focal column carries a hairline frame plus a named caption — no
  connectors, focus glow, lift, ranking, completion language, or special
  treatment for twelve holdings.
- One finished-state choreography, run once per composition: sources arrive
  separately, the Alignment settles, the focal caption lands, then sentence
  and reading (~1.1s, transform/opacity only). Reduced motion receives one
  finished-state fade with identical content.
- Produce 375px visual proofs for one, four, and twelve held signs from the
  production renderer.

### Independent signals

Each held sign may show either, both, or neither of these independent facts:

- **In the selected birth chart:** the sign contains the saved chart's Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, ASC, or MC.
- **In the sky of {date}:** the sign is the current Sun season, current Moon sign, or has an ingress, station, lunation, or eclipse within ±24 hours of the visit instant.

Jupiter through Pluto and the nodes may appear as secondary facts, but their residence alone never selects a sign. Both facts render independently and never form a ranked or canonical state.

Select one focal sign for the lead reading using: exact event → Moon → season → natal echo → zodiac order. This changes presentation only, never value.

### Result order and evidence

Every composed result renders in this order:

1. Identifier-free Chart / public-wallet-address / dated-Sky source docket
   (the chart plate switches to honest example labeling in example mode; a
   divergent wallet-check date gains a relative word and a re-check pointer).
2. The Alignment, whose row labels are the persistent three-source legend.
3. One factual sentence explaining the relationship (generated by the
   composer — one source of truth for real and example modes).
4. One grounded symbolic reading, amplified as the typographic peak (pastel
   disc, sign name, then a braided reading in plain everyday language: the
   sign's base line plus strands that quote the actual driving sky fact and
   the strongest echoing chart point, with absence stated honestly and the
   example never claiming “your” chart); “a talisman for this visit” may
   appear only as a qualified metaphor. The share card still paints only the
   natal-independent base line.
5. Collapsed evidence plates (“The evidence, sign by sign”).
6. Pre-share disclosure and sharing controls.

The docket states: “These sources are read side by side. The wallet record
never changes the birth chart; it only determines which held signs receive a
reading.” It never prints a chart name or address fragment. Use “Next in the
sky” plus the named event and date instead of user-facing activation language.

Keep the four evidence registers. `Record` and `Reading` always render; `Chart` and `Sky` render only when evidence exists. The example Aura is the first screen's centerpiece: a build-time, server-rendered Alignment with its reading, honestly stamped with its build-date sky and labeled illustrative on every source plate (including the chart plate: "Example chart · Built into this page · No chart was read from this browser"). It states that no public address was checked, cannot be shared, and does not emit meaningfulness feedback. An example escape stays visible from every failure state.

## Entry, wallet, and privacy

### Entry methods

Provide three explicit entry methods:

- Connect a Solana wallet through Wallet Standard.
- Connect a Base/EVM wallet through EIP-6963.
- Paste a public address.

Wallet connection occurs only after a click and requests only the selected public address. Aura must not import or call signing, approval, transaction, or chain-switching methods. Paste remains fully functional without a wallet extension.

Support one address and one chain per Aura in v1. Cross-chain merging remains future work. When a connected wallet changes account or disconnects, clear the composed result and require a fresh composition.

Use address-neutral language for pasted addresses: “this address carries.” After a wallet connection use “connected address.” Never claim verified identity or control.

### Beginner wallet education

Place a static, no-JavaScript wallet guide before the composer so a first-time visitor can understand the Record source before making a choice. Keep three paths visible: connect an installed wallet, paste a public address, or view the illustrative example without a wallet, balance, purchase, or saved chart.

The recovery-phrase warning is never collapsed. The guide must explain, in plain language:

- A wallet manages account keys while assets and activity remain recorded on a blockchain.
- A public address is shareable but traceable; it is not a password, identity proof, or control proof.
- A recovery phrase or private key can control assets and must never be entered into Aura, a website, a message, or support chat.
- Connecting and pasting produce the same read-only lookup; connection is only a convenience for selecting an address.
- Aura never needs signing, approval, sending, swapping, payment, or network switching. An unexpected request for any of these must be cancelled.
- Safe installation starts at the provider's exact official website or official app-store listing, not a search advertisement or direct-message link.
- Disconnecting and clearing Aura remove local composition data but do not erase public blockchain history or revoke unrelated token approvals.

Installation links are third-party orientation, not endorsements. Keep them on an explicit host allowlist with no tracking query. Do not add analytics for guide opens or wallet-install links.

### Chart bridge

Add a contextual Registry Aura link to the saved birth-chart result in v1. If no chart exists, Aura links to the calculator with an allowlisted return route. Birth data and chart-derived facts remain in the browser.

### Storage and disclosure

- The holdings endpoint receives only the public address.
- Disclose that the address is forwarded to the configured blockchain data provider and that routine request metadata may be processed.
- Keep the address, chain, held-sign result, check time, and selected local chart link in session storage by default, but make the record ineligible for restoration after eight hours. Delete expired bytes on Aura's next storage read and disclose that they may remain until the visitor returns or clears site data.
- Offer an unchecked “Remember this Aura on this device” option for the same record, eligible for restoration from local storage for 24 hours. The label must name the stored categories, both eligibility periods, and the deletion-on-next-read behavior.
- Put “Forget address / Clear Aura data” beside Refresh.
- Delete expired persisted data rather than merely marking it stale.
- Explain that Clear removes Aura's browser records but cannot erase public blockchain history, shared images, or infrastructure records retained under disclosed provider policies.

### Financial, third-party, and legal posture

- Keep Aura purchase-free. Before composition, state that it is a read-only symbolic display, not an investment signal or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold an asset, and that no purchase is required to use the example.
- Define “official Zodiac” narrowly: the mint or contract matches the Zodiacs.org Registry. The label is not government, regulatory, wallet-provider, or exchange approval and does not establish safety, value, liquidity, identity, or control.
- State that digital assets may be speculative, volatile, illiquid, affected by technical or regulatory change, and may lose all market value. Put the complete loss, irreversibility, independent-venue, and operator-interest disclosure beside every Registry market or acquisition route, not only in Terms or a footer.
- Wallets, app stores, explorers, RPC providers, bridges, venues, and sharing destinations are independent third parties with their own terms, privacy practices, and risks. Zodiacs.org does not execute, custody, reverse, recover, or guarantee their actions.
- Correct the canonical Privacy, Terms, and Disclosure pages before flag-on. They must describe Aura's address/provider flow, exact browser retention, analytics exclusions, sharing limits, wallet-connection posture, third-party boundaries, digital-asset risks, and the real legal operator/controller and governing jurisdiction.
- Operator holdings, related-project interests, creator or liquidity interests, paid promotion, referral compensation, and marketplace compensation require a dated factual attestation. Until that attestation exists, keep the economic-interest disclosure visibly pending and block launch.
- Name and link the deployed Solana and Base data providers and document their purpose, logging/retention, and transfer/subprocessor posture before flag-on. Do not claim that addresses are never logged without verified infrastructure guarantees.
- Do not claim that any Registry asset is or is not a security, is legally available everywhere, or is risk-free. Jurisdiction-specific digital-asset and privacy counsel review remains a launch prerequisite.

### Sharing

The default share is one 1080×1350 focal-sign card, not a full Carry Ring.
Its sign is selected from public sky facts only (exact event → Moon →
season → zodiac order); natal evidence never affects selection or the
default reflection. A session-only, default-off checkbox may add “Also in the
selected birth chart” when that already-selected sign has chart evidence. The
checkbox never changes the sign and plainly discloses cumulative chart-sign
privacy risk.

The card leads with the sign art and name, then the base reflection at
feed-legible size, then a three-row mini-ledger (private chart · read on a
device / focal sign found at a public wallet address with its checked date /
sky with the current Sun and Moon signs and date), “A symbolic reading — not
a wallet score or investment signal,” an optional named next sky event for
that same public focal sign, and the Registry Aura URL. The current Sun and
Moon signs are public facts, identical for every visitor at that instant.
Public calendar events are named from the committed catalog's structured
fields with their body (“Mercury stations direct”), never as sign-only
redactions. Exclude address, chain, chart name, birth data, *natal* bodies,
degrees, exact times, geometry, held count/list, houses, and aspects.
Renderer inputs are structured and allowlisted; arbitrary painted strings are
forbidden. Sharing remains an on-device PNG with Web Share and
download fallback, with no personalized URL. Generation first opens an inline
preview of the exact retained PNG blob; Web Share or download occurs only after
a second explicit action. Preview URLs are transient and revoked whenever the
preview closes or its result, focal evidence, or chart-disclosure input changes.

## Interfaces and implementation

### SDK alignment

The public `@zodiacs/sdk` exists. Aura keeps the site-local resolver for its production endpoint, while aligning sign order and identity semantics with the published SDK. Pin the SDK as a test-only conformance dependency; do not include it in the client bundle.

### Holdings endpoint

Add `POST /api/aura-holdings`.

Request:

```json
{ "address": "string" }
```

Success:

```json
{
  "chain": "solana",
  "heldSigns": ["aries"],
  "checkedAt": "2026-07-16T00:00:00.000Z"
}
```

`chain` is `solana` or `base`. Errors use `invalid_address`, `forbidden`, `disabled`, `method`, `rate_limited`, or `unavailable`. Responses never echo the address. Successful signs are unique and in zodiac order; a successful empty lookup is `200` with `heldSigns: []`.

Solana queries only the twelve official Registry mints; it never retrieves unrelated SPL holdings for later filtering. Provider errors, timeout, malformed data, or partial Solana or Base batch results return `503`; partial data never becomes a false absence. Enforce serialized body size, same-origin policy, provider timeout, private/no-store response headers, and platform rate limiting. Production uses Vercel Firewall's `@vercel/firewall` counter with rule ID `registry-aura-holdings-v1`; launch is blocked until the matching WAF rule is published.

### Client model

Derived client data uses:

```ts
type AuraComposition = {
  heldSigns: SignSlug[];
  signs: Array<{
    sign: SignSlug;
    natalEcho: NatalEvidence[];
    activeNow: ActiveEvidence[];
    nextActivation: { kind: string; sign: SignSlug; at: string; source: string } | null;
    uncertainties: AuraUncertainty[];
    reading: AuraReading;
  }>;
  uncertainties: AuraUncertainty[];
};
```

There is no four-level canonical state enum.

Compute the current Sun and Moon locally at the visit instant with the lightweight engine. Use committed event files for ingresses, stations, lunations, and eclipses, with explicit freshness validation. Exact future Moon activations come from a separate committed Moon-ingress table generated by the full astronomy engine; the lightweight Moon model must not present a bisected timestamp as exact. Event sources follow the repository's yearly rebuild cadence and fail closed after a bounded 400-day freshness window or outside their declared coverage.

For an unknown birth time:

- omit Moon, ASC, MC, houses, and every exact natal degree from Aura evidence;
- use sign-level Sun, Mercury, Venus, and Mars evidence;
- show a visible noon-estimate note.

Use a simplified natal wheel without cusps or aspects. Do not load the full astronomy engine in the eager Aura bundle.

### Registry entry and feature flag

Update both Registry landing representations: the hydrated source and the no-JavaScript pre-render. Regenerate their shared output together.

One build-time feature flag controls:

- the Aura route;
- Registry entries;
- the birth-chart-result link;
- sitemap inclusion.

Flag-on and flag-off builds must contain no dangling links and pass the same verification suite.

### Analytics

Capture only coarse events:

- view;
- compose outcome and held-count bucket;
- share;
- refresh;
- calculator round-trip;
- main-site entry conversion;
- coarse return interval;
- optional “Meaningful / Not yet” response.

Do not track addresses, chart IDs, birth fields, exact held-sign lists, clicked sign identity, or connected account identifiers.
Held-count buckets are exactly `0 | 1 | 2-5 | 6-12`.

## Verification and rollout

### Unit tests

- Natal-echo inclusion and excluded generational placements.
- Sun, Moon, and event activation boundaries.
- Combined chart/sky evidence without ranking.
- Exact next-sky-event selection.
- Unknown-time omissions and uncertainty copy.
- Determinism and stale-sky behavior.

### Endpoint tests

- Solana and Base success and empty holdings.
- Within-chain uniqueness and zodiac ordering.
- Invalid address, oversized body, cross-origin, disabled, timeout, rate limit, and unsupported method.
- Partial Solana or Base RPC batches return unavailable.
- No provider URL or address echo appears in responses.

### Connector tests

- Connections occur only after interaction.
- Only public-address APIs are called.
- Account changes and disconnects invalidate results.
- Paste works without an installed wallet.
- Bundle inspection finds no signing, approval, or transaction calls.
- Wallet education remains available with JavaScript disabled, precedes the composer, and opening it triggers no account or holdings request.
- Native disclosures work with Enter and Space, retain focus, remain at least 44px tall, and do not overflow at 320px with every section open.
- Safety copy covers public-address traceability, secret recovery material, unexpected prompt cancellation, no-purchase example access, and clearing versus revocation.
- External installation and security links use only approved HTTPS hosts, contain no tracking query, and isolate new tabs.

### Privacy and share tests

- No request contains birth or chart data.
- Only the holdings endpoint contains the entered address.
- Session data clears correctly.
- Remembering is explicit; Clear removes session and persistent data.
- Privacy copy names every Aura storage category and stays contract-aligned with the eight-hour session and 24-hour remembered TTLs.
- Solana provider requests contain only the twelve official mints; unrelated token accounts are never returned to or processed by the Aura resolver.
- Default PNG focus and painted strings are independent of natal evidence.
- Chart disclosure is session-only, off by default, and never changes the card sign.
- Pre-share copy states that a destination or recipient may retain, copy, or repost a shared image and that Clear cannot recall it.
- Canvas text and Web Share payload contain no address, chain, chart name, natal body, degree, exact time, held count/list, geometry, arbitrary reflection, house, or aspect data. Public catalog bodies and the current Sun/Moon signs render only through structured, allowlisted builders.
- Creating a card invokes neither Web Share nor download; the preview is the exact 1080×1350 PNG later shared or downloaded, stale preview URLs are revoked on every input/result change, and unsupported Web Share leaves the reviewed download path available.

### Legal and disclosure tests

- Canonical and localized disclosure pages never claim that the Registry cannot connect wallets; they distinguish public-address access from signatures, approvals, payments, and transactions.
- Privacy, Terms, Disclosure, hydrated Registry, no-JavaScript Registry output, and all twelve generated record pages contain their required risk and third-party boundaries with no contradictory “read-only” acquisition claim.
- Every acquisition route places total-loss, illiquidity, independent-venue, irreversibility, and no-recommendation language adjacent to the outbound action.
- “Official” is defined as Registry matching only, and never as government approval, safety, value, liquidity, identity, or control.
- Provider identity/retention and operator economic-interest attestations are verified as launch prerequisites rather than invented by code or copy.

### UI tests

- Focus moves safely from compose to the result heading and returns on error.
- Live regions announce lookup and composition outcomes.
- Reduced motion, forced colors, 200%/400% zoom, mobile layouts, offline, stale cache, no chart, no holdings, and unavailable-provider states work.
- New 375px proofs cover one, four, and twelve holdings. At 250px card width, the sign plus `BIRTH CHART`, `WALLET ADDRESS`, `SKY`, and the public-address fact remain readable.

### Build tests

Flag-off and flag-on builds pass tests, type checks, bundle budgets, schema validation, link checks, sitemap checks, legacy-output drift checks, and visual/Lighthouse verification.

### Rollout

Launch behind the independent Aura feature flag with endpoint invocation and error-rate monitoring. Rollback disables the route and every entry point together.
Keep the flag off until ordinary, crypto-literate, and astrology-literate viewers each pass the five-second comprehension test: they can identify the private chart, public wallet record, and dated sky as separate inputs, and describe the output as a symbolic reading rather than a wallet score.

## Deferred scope

Compatibility, two-address merging, Simastry export, element/modality summaries, historical holding duration, push notifications, and Vitrine time travel remain post-v1.

## Success criterion

Success means visitors understand Chart / Record / Sky / Reading, find at least one result meaningful, and return or share around a later sky event—not merely that the integration works.
