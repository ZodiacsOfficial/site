The feature

How to buy your sign in fomo.

All twelve Zodiacs are in the fomo app. Here is how buying one works, from download to your first sign.

The fomo app is free on iPhone, Android, and the web, and is made by FOMO Labs, a company independent of Zodiacs.org. You sign in with an email address or Apple ID, and fomo sets up a wallet inside the app.

You add funds with Apple Pay where it is offered, or send money in from a wallet or exchange you already use. Starting with a small amount is sensible: Zodiac tokens are speculative, and their prices can fall as fast as they rise.

Every Buy with Fomo button on this page opens your sign in fomo directly, using its verified address from the Registry, so you never have to pick it out of a list of lookalikes.
