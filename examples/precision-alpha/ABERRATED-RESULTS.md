# validated-retarded-aberrated — what was measured

Companion to `ABERRATED-PREREGISTRATION.md`, which declared every case,
tolerance and pass rule below before any of them ran, and to
`RETARDED-RESULTS.md`, which records the light-time operation this one
extends. That document and its numbers stand unchanged.

Run 2026-09-21.

---

## 1 · What the operation is

    d(t)  = r_target(t - tau) - r_observer(t)        reception light-time
    tau   = |d(t)| / c                               solved as a verified contraction
    u(t)  = aberrate( d(t), v_observer(t)/c )        stellar aberration
    f(t)  = sin(L) u_x - cos(L) (cos(e0) u_y + sin(e0) u_z) = 0
    g(t)  = cos(L) u_x + sin(L) (cos(e0) u_y + sin(e0) u_z) > 0

The observer is at reception time throughout, in position AND in velocity,
and is never retarded. `e0` is 84381.406 arcsec. Time is TDB seconds past
J2000 in and out, with no conversion inside the operation.

**Not an apparent place.** Gravitational deflection, the Klioner
solar-potential term inside the aberration itself, the Shapiro delay,
precession, nutation, the IAU 2006 frame bias and everything topocentric
are all absent, and every result lists them by name under
`diagnostics.notApplied`. The name says what it computes, which is why it
is not called `validated-apparent-of-date`.

## 2 · The enclosure, and the two things that do not carry over

`aberrate` normalizes twice: once to make its input a unit vector, once to
make its output one. The search never needs a unit vector — it needs the
sign of two projections, both linear in the direction — so multiplying
through by |d| > 0 removes both:

    P = bm1 d + S v,      S = |d| + (d . v) / (1 + bm1),      u = P/|P|

and `w . u = 0` iff `w . P = 0`, with `sign(w . u) = sign(w . P)`, because
|P| > 0 is checked rather than assumed. Two interval divisions disappear
with the normalizations, which matters: `div` refuses outright when a
denominator straddles zero, so each one removed is a way the enclosure can
no longer fail. Setting v = 0 gives P = d exactly, and
`AB-R1`/`AB-R2` assert that reduction on the arithmetic and on a whole
search rather than leaving it to this paragraph.

Two things do **not** carry over, and both were checked rather than
assumed:

* **The derivative.** F' is not f'·|P|. Rescaling a function by a quantity
  that itself varies does not rescale its derivative, so the positive
  factor that was free to drop above is not free here. P' is derived
  directly from P as written, through |d|', bm1' and S'.
* **The monotonicity test.** The light-time mode pairs a midpoint
  derivative with a Lipschitz constant for it, which needs |f''| and so
  the target's acceleration. The aberrated F'' needs the OBSERVER's jerk
  as well, and the stored series are not differentiated that far. So this
  mode establishes monotonicity the direct way: an interval enclosure of
  F' over the whole cell that excludes zero. Both are sound; neither is a
  relaxation of the other, and the measured cost is nil (section 5).

Domain conditions established over each interval, not at sampled instants:
|v| < 1 throughout, |d| > 0, |P| > 0, 1 − |v|² bounded above zero, and the
coefficient-record boundaries the emission window crosses.

## 4 · The control ladder

Every holdout case ran all three rungs on identical instants, the same
observer and target, the same frame and the same data. The last column of
the table above is rung 3 minus rung 2. In **every** case it equals the
shift the independent reference predicts, to the precision printed — the
harness asserts agreement to 2 × 10⁻³ s, and the tier-B suite repeats the
assertion.

The size of the shift is set by how fast the body's geocentric longitude
moves, not by the aberration angle, which is about twenty arcseconds for
all of them:

## 5 · Cost

The direct monotonicity test costs nothing measurable. On the synthetic
Earth-like/Mars-like geometry, aberrated against light-time: 2 082 against
2 082 at one longitude and 2 082 against 2 081 at another. On the holdout's
Moon case, 296 004 against the light-time holdout's 296 182.
With the observer at rest the aberrated mode returns the light-time mode's
root, bracket and evaluation count exactly.

Worst observer speed any accepted cell admitted: **1.67 × 10⁻⁴** in units
of c. That is above Earth's 9.9 × 10⁻⁵ because the figure is the top of a
mean-value enclosure over a whole cell, not an instantaneous value.

## 6 · Where the reported midpoint is not inside 10⁻³ s

Near a turning point the operation reports a bracket wider than the
tolerance, and the midpoint of that bracket can sit outside it. This is
not a holdout failure — no holdout case does it — but it is real and it is
reachable, so it is stated here rather than left to be discovered.

At a stationary point f' goes to zero, the sign of f stops being separable
from its own enclosure while the bracket is still wide, and the bisection
stops there rather than claiming a sign it cannot establish. Measured on
the synthetic retrograde geometry: **2.1 × 10⁻² s** of bracket at the turn
against **8.1 × 10⁻⁵ s** for a transversal crossing of the same geometry,
and a midpoint 1.9 × 10⁻³ s from the reference root.

Attributed, not guessed: a reference built from the pack's OWN polynomial
agrees with the ideal-circle reference to 1.4 × 10⁻⁶ s on those cases, so
the width is the search declining to resolve a tangency and not the
Chebyshev fit. **Containment holds throughout**, and containment is the
claim the operation makes — `bracketTdbSec` and `bracketWidthSec` are on
every event, and `tdbSec` is the midpoint of the first, not an
independently accurate number.

## 8 · Two defects the cases found

**A superluminal observer spent the whole budget reaching a refusal it
could reach at once.** 199 883 cells and 4 000 001 evaluations to say what
the first cell already showed — the same defect the light-time mode
carries a note about for a superluminal target. `aberrateInterval` now
separates "above c at every instant this cell admits", where halving
changes nothing, from "the enclosure straddles c", where it can. The 1.4c
case costs 462 evaluations and finishes. Exactly c falls in the second
case, because the stated widening puts its enclosure across the line, and
that is the honest classification rather than a special case.

**The packed archive could be installed but the experimental mode could
not be run.** `examples/synthetic-pack.mjs` shipped in `files` but had no
`exports` entry, so a consumer importing it got
ERR_PACKAGE_PATH_NOT_EXPORTED — and while real coefficient packs cannot be
distributed, that fixture is the only way to run the mode at all. Every
one of the 268 tests imports by relative path from inside the repository,
which resolves nothing the way a consumer does.
`tools/consumer/clean-consumer.mjs` now packs, installs offline into an
empty project and runs a consumer that imports by specifier only.

Two fixture defects of my own are recorded in the commit that fixed them:
an aliasing case that fitted 5.3 cycles into 20 Chebyshev terms (30 930 km
of fit error, so it tested the fit and not the search), and a test that
expected exactly c to be classified hopeless.

## 10 · What this does not establish

Completeness here is completeness **about the stored polynomial model with
these two corrections applied**. It is not a statement about the sky, not
an apparent place, and not a chart. Agreement with any external ephemeris
lies outside every bound set here, and section 1's omission list is what
stands between this and an apparent place — deflection, the Shapiro delay,
precession, nutation, the frame bias and everything topocentric. None of
them was begun.

The reference's own completeness is bounded by its scan step, declared per
body and not chosen by the run; two roots closer than one step could be
invisible to it. The ERFA comparison settles the pointwise transformation
at 2.6 × 10⁻¹¹ arcsec and is a pointwise comparison, not an interval
bound — the enclosure's soundness rests on the interval arithmetic and its
stated widening, not on that agreement.
