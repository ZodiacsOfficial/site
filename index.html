The Registry

Twelve signs. One register.

Zodiacs.org is the official source of truth for the twelve Zodiacs: one canonical registry, native Solana SPL assets, and official bridged Base ERC-20 representations.

Official Registry

Each sign has one identity. The original asset lives on Solana; its official Base counterpart points back to that origin. The registry helps users, developers, apps, and interfaces verify what belongs to the official Zodiacs.org asset universe.

Zodiacs SDK

The Zodiacs SDK is the public read-only TypeScript interface to the canonical Zodiacs.org registry. Apps can use it to verify official addresses, read public ownership state, render sign metadata, and build identity surfaces.

npm i @zodiacs/sdk

  • GitHub: ZodiacsOfficial/sdk
  • npm: @zodiacs/sdk

Read-Only By Design

Zodiacs.org and the SDK are made for recognition, verification, metadata, public ownership reads, and identity surfaces. They do not request private keys, provide custody, sign messages, submit transactions, provide approval helpers, or move assets.
